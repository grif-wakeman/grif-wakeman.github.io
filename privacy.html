Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information Sync Beast collects on this website and how we use it. It covers syncbeast.xyz and every page on it, including the Mentorship page.

What we collect

  • What you enter in our application form, such as your name, email address, and answers about your music and goals.
  • Your name and email address when you book a call or email us.
  • Anything you choose to share with us on a call or by email.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you load a page, such as your IP address and browser type.

Services that handle your data

This site uses a few outside services to work. Each one may receive basic technical data when you load a page:

  • Typeform runs our application form and stores what you submit in it.
  • Font Awesome, Google Fonts, and unpkg deliver icons, fonts, and the audio player.
  • Our web host serves the site.

These services have their own privacy policies. Typeform may set cookies to run the form. We don't set our own tracking or advertising cookies on this site.

How we use your information

  • To review your application.
  • To schedule and hold your call.
  • To answer your questions and follow up about our programs.
  • To deliver the program or mentorship if you join.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information. We don't share it except with the services we use to run the business, such as forms, scheduling, email, and video calls.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time. Email us and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date.
